# SOC Analyst Workflow

## Overview

A Security Operations Center (SOC) monitors, detects, investigates, and responds to security threats.

A SOC Analyst is responsible for reviewing alerts, analyzing evidence, determining whether activity is malicious, and escalating confirmed incidents.

## Alert Investigation Process

A typical SOC investigation follows these stages:

```text
Alert
  ↓
Initial Triage
  ↓
Collect Evidence
  ↓
Analyze & Correlate
  ↓
Determine Severity
  ↓
True Positive / False Positive
  ↓
Contain / Escalate
  ↓
Document
