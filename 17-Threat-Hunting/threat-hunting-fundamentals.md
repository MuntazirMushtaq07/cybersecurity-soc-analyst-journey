# Threat Hunting Fundamentals

## Overview

Threat hunting is the proactive process of searching for suspicious or malicious activity that may not have triggered a security alert.

Unlike alert-driven investigation, threat hunting starts with a hypothesis or suspicious behavior and searches available data for evidence.
